1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you interact with our website, submit enquiry forms, request property viewings, or sign up for our Investor Academy, we may collect:

  • Full name, email address, phone number
  • Country of residence and nationality
  • Investment budget range and property preferences
  • Messages and correspondence you send to us

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs
  • Device identifiers and screen resolution
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 5)

Third-Party Information

We may receive information about you from third-party sources, including:

  • Property listing platforms and partner agencies
  • Social media platforms (if you interact with our profiles)
  • Analytics and advertising partners
  • Publicly available sources relevant to property transactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide property consultation services
  • Schedule and coordinate property viewings
  • Send you relevant property recommendations based on your stated preferences
  • Process transactions and facilitate property purchases
  • Deliver educational content through our Investor Academy
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations, including UAE real estate regulations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our services

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Third parties that help us operate our website, process transactions, and deliver services (e.g., hosting, analytics, email platforms)
  • Property developers and partners: When necessary to facilitate a property transaction you have initiated
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: When required by law, including the Dubai Land Department (DLD) and the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA)
  • Professional advisors: Lawyers, accountants, and auditors as necessary for business operations

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and understand user behaviour. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign performance

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect your experience on our website.

5. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our mailing list, submit an enquiry, or sign up for the Investor Academy, we may send you:

  • Property updates and market insights relevant to your interests
  • Academy course materials and educational content
  • Company news and service updates

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email or by contacting us directly. Please note that we may still send you transactional or service-related communications.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS), secure server infrastructure, access controls, and regular security assessments.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When personal information is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

For property transaction records, we may retain information for a minimum period as required by UAE law and RERA regulations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at customercare@fairopportunityrealestate.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10. UAE Data Protection

Fair Opportunity Real Estate operates in accordance with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and all applicable data protection regulations in the United Arab Emirates. We are committed to ensuring that our data processing activities comply with local laws and best practices.

As a RERA-regulated entity (Broker No: 31740), we also adhere to all data handling requirements set forth by the Dubai Land Department and the Real Estate Regulatory Agency.

By using our services, you consent to the transfer of your information to the UAE, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.
