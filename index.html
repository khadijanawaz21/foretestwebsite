Golden Visa

Your investment.
Your residency.

The UAE Golden Visa isn't just a residence permit — it's a 10-year renewable visa that gives you and your family the freedom to live, work, and build in the UAE. Property investment is one of the most direct paths to qualifying.

  • 10-year renewable residency for you, your spouse, and dependents
  • No requirement to stay in the UAE — travel freely on your terms
  • Open UAE bank accounts and access the full financial ecosystem
  • Qualify with a property purchase from AED 2,000,000
  • Zero income tax. Zero capital gains tax. Zero inheritance tax.
