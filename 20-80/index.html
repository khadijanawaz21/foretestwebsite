Questions

Frequently asked.

How does the 20/80 payment plan work?
You pay 10% at booking, 40% during construction according to the project's payment schedule, and the remaining 50% at handover — when the property is complete and the keys are handed over.
When is the 50% payable?
The final 50% is due at handover, once the property is completed and ready for key transfer. The exact date depends on the individual project's construction timeline — we'll confirm this for any property before you commit.
What properties are available?
Availability changes regularly as new phases release and existing units sell. Send us your budget and preferences via the form or WhatsApp and we'll share the current options that match on the 20/80 plan.
Is this suitable for investors and end users?
Yes. The 20/80 structure is used by both investors, who benefit from spreading capital across the construction period, and end users planning to move in at handover. What's right for you depends on your goals and timeline — an advisor can help you think it through.
Are all projects on the same payment plan?
No. Payment plans vary by developer and project — some offer 20/80, others use different structures. We'll confirm the exact payment plan for any specific property before you commit to anything.
Can I get current prices and availability?
Yes — submit the form below or message us on WhatsApp and an advisor will send you current, verified prices and availability for properties on the 20/80 plan.
What happens after I enquire?
A FORE advisor will reach out — typically within 24 hours — to understand your budget and goals, then share the specific properties and payment schedules that match.
